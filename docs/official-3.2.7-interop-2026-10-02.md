The Web Audio specification states that low-pass and high-pass `BiquadFilterNode.Q` is expressed in dB and uses the linear equivalent `10^(Q/20)`. The initial .NET vent and radio filters had incorrectly passed the upstream dB values directly to NAudio's linear-Q biquad. They now convert 20 dB to 10 for the vent low-pass and 10 dB to approximately 3.162 for the radio high-pass. Camera reception from `spatialAudio.ts` is now implemented for the Airship, Polus, and Skeld camera coordinates from `AmongusMap.ts`: when a speaker is beyond normal range but near the selected camera, it uses camera-relative attenuation/panning, 80% unmodified gain, and a 2.3 kHz low-pass with -15 dB Q. Policy and synthetic-frequency tests pass, as does a zero-warning WPF build. Live audible A/B verification remains outstanding.

The `wallsBlockAudio` policy now uses the exact v3.2.7 `ColliderMap.ts` wall and closed-door path data, embedded as JSON. Its SVG paths are parsed once; cubic curves are subdivided to 0.001 map-unit flatness before segment intersection. Normal task proximity is blocked for living listeners, while meetings, radio, ghosts, and camera reception follow the upstream exceptions. Self-tests cover Fungle walls, Skeld and Airship doors, the mirrored April Skeld, and policy exceptions; the WPF build is warning-free. The subdivision is a close numerical port of `path-intersection`, not yet a proof of identical behavior at every curve boundary. Live game testing with the lobby option enabled is outstanding.
